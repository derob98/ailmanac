<LevelBadge level="beginner" />

Paste any prompt or document and get a quick token estimate — so you can budget context window and cost *before* you hit the API. All client-side; nothing leaves your browser.

<TokenEstimator />

:::tip[Why tokens matter]
Context limits and pricing are both measured in tokens (≈ ¾ of a word). Trimming what you send cuts both cost and latency. Deep dive in [Tokens, Context & Pricing](/docs/api/tokens-and-pricing).
:::
