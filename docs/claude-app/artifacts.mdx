Artifacts are available on **Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise**. The three *templates* — **Claude Docs**, **Claude Slides** and **Claude Design** — are **in beta on paid plans only**: on by default on Pro, Max and Team, and off on Enterprise until an owner turns each one on. Capabilities here move fast — confirm current behavior in the Help Center.
</VerifyNote>

**Artifacts** are outputs Claude renders in a **side panel** next to the chat — a document, a deck, a chart, a working app — that you can see, use, and iterate on, separate from the conversation text.

Since the [September 2026 merge](/docs/claude-app/one-claude-docs-and-slides), the panel is also where the three **templates** live. That makes "artifact" the container and "template" the starting shape.

## What you can make

| Output | What it is | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mini web apps & tools** | A calculator, a quiz, a form, a small interactive demo | All plans |
| **Charts & diagrams** | Visuals and simple data dashboards | All plans |
| **Code** | Something you can read and run | All plans |
| **Claude Docs** | A living, multi-tab document you co-edit with Claude and your team | Paid, beta |
| **Claude Slides** | A deck built from your notes or the conversation, presentable in place | Paid, beta |
| **Claude Design** | Visuals, mockups, prototypes and landing pages against your design system | Paid, beta |

Exports differ by type: Docs go to **Word, PDF, Markdown and Google Docs**; Slides to **PowerPoint and PDF**; Designs as a **.zip, PDF, PowerPoint or standalone HTML**.

## Why it's powerful for non-developers

You can build something *usable* — "make me a tip calculator for a group dinner," "a dashboard from this CSV" — by describing it, then refine it conversationally ("add a service-charge field," "make the buttons bigger"). It's the clearest example of **building with AI without writing code yourself**.

## Tips

- **Be concrete** about inputs/outputs and audience — same as good [prompting](/docs/prompting/basics).
- **Iterate small.** One change at a time is easier to get right.
- **Verify any logic/numbers** an artifact computes for important uses ([Hallucinations](/docs/foundations/hallucinations)).
- **Charts inside a Doc don't refresh themselves.** Change the data and the chart keeps the old values until you ask Claude to rebuild it.
